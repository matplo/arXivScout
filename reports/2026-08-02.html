Heavy-Ion Collision Papers

Experiment

Probing (Hyper)Nuclei Wave Functions and Production Mechanisms in sqrt(sNN)=200 GeV Isobar Collisions at RHIC

strong

STAR Collaboration | arXiv:2607.28113 | carried forward from the July 31 screened batch

Summary: STAR reports centrality-dependent production of hypertriton, anti-hypertriton, helium-3, anti-helium-3, and triton in 200 GeV Ru+Ru and Zr+Zr collisions. The measured ratios deviate from thermal-model expectations and are better described by coalescence calculations with realistic non-Gaussian wave functions.

Why it matters: The result uses heavy-ion production as a quantitative probe of hypernuclear wave functions and hyperon-nucleon interactions.

Critical assessment: Strong. The observable-model connection is still coalescence-dependent, but this is real experimental leverage rather than a purely phenomenological fit exercise.

Phenomenology / AI/ML Use

Unbiased Data-Driven Determination of the Nuclear Dipole Amplitude in the Color Glass Condensate

strong

Si-Wei Dai, Haowu Duan, Long-Gang Pang, Guang-You Qin, Shu-Yi Wei, Han-Zhong Zhang, Wenbin Zhao (7 authors) | arXiv:2607.27603 | carried forward from the July 31 screened batch

Summary: The paper embeds collinearly improved BK evolution and positivity constraints into a physics-informed neural extraction of the Pb dipole amplitude from forward-hadron nuclear-modification and coherent J/psi photoproduction data.

Why it matters: It is a serious example of ML used as constrained inference for small-x nuclear structure, with transfer value for pA baselines, EIC phenomenology, and heavy-ion initial conditions.

Critical assessment: Strong, but not assumption-free. The phrase "unbiased" should be read as reduced functional-parametrization bias under explicit QCD-evolution and positivity assumptions.

Theory

Relativistic transport near moving interfaces

mixed

Lorenzo Gavassino (1 author) | arXiv:2607.28569 | carried forward from the July 31 screened batch

Summary: This formal work studies linear disturbances near moving planar interfaces in relativistic media, including boundary layers, wakes, and asymptotic shock tails.

Why it matters: It could inform heavy-ion modeling of wake and shock-like response, though the connection to realistic QGP geometries remains indirect.

Critical assessment: Mathematically interesting and likely sound, but presently more framework than immediately deployable phenomenology.

AI/ML Use

CLVisc Agent for autonomous relativistic hydrodynamics studies

mixed

Qi Wang, Long-Gang Pang, Shi Pu, Xin-Nian Wang (4 authors) | arXiv:2607.27822 | carried forward from the July 31 screened batch

Summary: The authors demonstrate an LLM-agent workflow that operates CLVisc, runs hydrodynamic scans, compares outputs, and produces figures for eta/s and O+O nuclear-structure studies.

Why it matters: It points toward reproducible automation for hydrodynamic campaigns and simulation bookkeeping.

Critical assessment: Mixed. Useful as a workflow prototype, but its scientific strength depends on robustness, uncertainty handling, and expert validation under less curated conditions.

AI/ML Use Cases in Physics

A Lightweight Foundation Model for Collider Physics with Multi-Domain Adaptation

mixed

Liangyu Wu, Qibin Liu, Alexander Yue, Julia Gonski (4 authors) | arXiv:2607.27501 | carried forward from the July 31 screened batch

Method and result: NEXUS pretrains a small autoencoder on charged-particle track features and fine-tunes it for collider regression and event-classification tasks with limited labels.

Likely use case: Credible transfer to low-label HEP tasks, online/edge inference studies, and possibly heavy-ion event representations if occupancy and domain-shift stress tests are added.

Critical assessment: Promising but still early. The key missing proof is robustness across detector conditions, pileup/occupancy regimes, and analysis-specific systematics.

DoTime: A Synthetic Benchmark Generator for Interventional and Counterfactual Time Series

mixed

Dennis Thumm, Billy Tim Anthony, Ying Chen (3 authors) | arXiv:2607.27263 | carried forward from the July 31 screened batch

Method and result: DoTime creates temporal structural causal model benchmarks with interventions, counterfactuals, regime switches, nonstationarity, and exact ground truth.

Likely use case: Transfer value is indirect but credible for detector operations, run-quality monitoring, and causal modeling of changing experimental conditions.

Critical assessment: Mixed-positive as a benchmark. It becomes valuable for HEP only if domain-realistic time-series generators and detector-condition variables are built on top.

Weak or Overclaimed Papers Worth Caution

Photon-calibrated event-activity bias and subcollision geometry in d+Au collisions at sqrt(sNN)=200 GeV with PYTHIA 8 Angantyr

caution

Muhammad Ajaz, Haifa I. Alrebdi, Muhammad Waqas (3 authors) | arXiv:2607.27572 | carried forward from the July 31 screened batch

Why caution: The study is useful as a generator-level diagnostic of event-activity bias in d+Au, but the abstract itself notes that it does not identify a unique microscopic origin for the residual correlations. It should not be overread as a data-constrained statement about small-system collectivity or hard-soft dynamics.
