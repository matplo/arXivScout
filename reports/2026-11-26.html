Nuclear Modification of π⁰ Production in pO and OO Collisions with ALICE

Experiment · Nicolas Strangmann · arXiv:2611.34503

The double ratio R_OO/R_pO² is suppressed relative to a cold-matter baseline by 4.9 standard deviations. This is a proceedings account of the earlier ALICE result, not a new independent measurement.

Assessment: Strong experimental relevance, but the cancellation of cold-matter effects depends on the model and kinematic matching; the measurement does not distinguish energy-loss mechanisms.

Abstract summary: The proceedings presents neutral-pion nuclear modification measurements in pO and OO collisions and compares the results with cold and hot nuclear matter calculations.

Accelerating ALICE Background Simulations: A WGAN-GP Approach to Slow-Neutron-Induced TPC Loopers

AI/ML use · Marco Giacalone, Sandro Christian Wenzel, Mesut Arslandok, Andreas Morsch · arXiv:2611.34102

Integrated into ALICE O2 production for pp and Pb–Pb, the model reduces the tested transport time from 21.5 s to about 2.0 s per event.

Assessment: Strong practical use case with production deployment and full-simulation comparisons. The looper rate is normalized separately, momentum tails need improvement, and the speedup is for transport, not end-to-end processing.

Abstract summary: A generative model replaces slow-neutron transport with generated electron and positron backgrounds for ALICE simulation, preserving key looper distributions and accelerating transport.

Equilibrium baseline for transverse-momentum correlations in Au+Au collisions at 3–39 GeV

Theory · Oleh Savchuk · arXiv:2611.34562

The calculation underpredicts STAR correlations by factors of 3–4 in central collisions at 11.5–39 GeV and becomes negative at 3–5.2 GeV, where the data are positive.

Assessment: Useful explicit baseline and direct data comparison. The discrepancy does not establish a critical point: freeze-out assumptions, acceptance, and nonequilibrium and flow fluctuations matter.

Abstract summary: An equilibrium hadron-resonance-gas calculation includes decays, quantum statistics, collective flow and conservation laws in a baseline for transverse-momentum correlations.

Introducing Parton-Level Fluctuations in the TRENTo-Sea Model

Theory · Jacob Reinach, Andreas Kirchner · arXiv:2611.33662

The model reproduces eccentricity from calibrated models except in central collisions, and finds that low-x parton populations affect local geometry.

Assessment: Promising initial-state framework rather than direct validation against final-state measurements. The infrared regulator is calibrated to pp cross sections and central-collision disagreement remains.

Abstract summary: TRENTo-Sea samples parton momentum fractions from nuclear PDFs and models parton-level interactions to study initial geometry and granularity.
