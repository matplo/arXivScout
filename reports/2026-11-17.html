Open Heavy Flavor in Nuclear Collisions: From Lattice QCD to Observables

Theory | Yu Fu, Steffen A. Bass, Tharun Krishna, Ralf Rapp, Weiyao Ke | arXiv:2611.21601

A lattice-constrained T-matrix transport framework combines Langevin diffusion, radiation, two hadronization schemes and hadronic rescattering. It compares D mesons and charm baryons with RHIC and LHC suppression, flow and yield-ratio data.

Assessment: Broad comparisons and component studies make this a particularly useful framework paper. Lattice constraints do not eliminate phenomenological assumptions in radiation, hadronization and late rescattering; agreement is not a unique extraction of diffusion.

Third-body-corrected Two-Pion Femtoscopic Correlations in Au+Au Collisions at High Baryon Density

Experiment | Youquan Qi (for the STAR Collaboration) | arXiv:2611.21284

STAR pion correlations from 3.0 to 7.7 GeV show charge-dependent HBT radii becoming consistent after a third-body Coulomb correction. The authors report a residual relative difference below 5% at 95% confidence.

Assessment: A valuable control of a potential background to isospin-sensitive femtoscopy. This is a short proceedings contribution; the bound depends on the effective Coulomb correction and its uncertainties, including extrapolated potentials.

Observation of Strong Directed Flow for phi Mesons in the High Baryon Density Region at RHIC

Experiment | Guangyu Zheng | arXiv:2611.20400

STAR fixed-target Au+Au results show phi-meson directed flow comparable in magnitude to proton and Lambda flow and larger than K-short flow at high baryon density. UrQMD comparisons are consistent with baryon-resonance production and baryonic mean-field effects.

Assessment: A useful species-dependent constraint on strange-hadron dynamics. The proposed production mechanism is model-supported, not uniquely established by the comparison.

Study on net-proton number fluctuations in Au+Au collisions at 7.7-27 GeV with the SMASH model

Phenomenology | Fengxi Yan, Ting Liu, Rongrong Ma, Yunshuang Qin, Shule Wu, Shenghui Zhang, Fan Si | arXiv:2611.20189

SMASH calculations provide a noncritical baseline for cumulant ratios with STAR-like acceptance and centrality treatment. At 19.6 GeV the central C4/C2 data-model pull is about -2.7, compared with about -4.0 against UrQMD under the authors' uncertainty prescription.

Assessment: An informative test of reference-model dependence, not a critical-point discovery. SMASH also systematically overshoots peripheral measurements; the diagnostic pull assumes independent uncertainty components.

Nuclear Mott transition with neural quantum states

Heavy-ion AI/ML | Zi-Xiao Zhang, Xiao-Lu Qian, Wan-Bing He, Gerd Röpke, Kai-Jia Sun, Yu-Gang Ma | arXiv:2611.20160

A self-attention neural quantum state solves an in-medium few-body problem and is benchmarked against exact diagonalization for the deuteron. The authors predict much weaker alpha suppression and large abundance changes compared with approximate calculations.

Assessment: A substantive use of ML on a difficult microscopic problem relevant to heavy-ion cluster yields. The exact benchmark is for the two-body system, not independent validation of the four-body result; the claimed explanation of collision data remains model-dependent.

Amortized conditional inference under changing parameter information in small-angle X-ray scattering

AI/ML in physics | Maria Bånkestad, Sandra Barman, Erik Kaunisto, Eskil Andreasson, Smita Chakraborty, Shun Yu, Jerk Rönnols, Sepideh Pashami | arXiv:2611.18773

Masked flow matching estimates conditional posteriors under changing parameter information without retraining. The study checks analytic posteriors, compares architectures in a simulated scattering benchmark and analyzes experimental SAXS ambiguities.

Assessment: Potential transfer: recondition heavy-ion simulation-based inference on changing nuisance constraints. This is a concrete methodological analogy, not a demonstrated HEP application; calibration and simulator mismatch would need dedicated validation.
