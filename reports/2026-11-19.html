Phenomenology | strong

The production of lepton pairs in heavy-ion collisions at SPS and RHIC energies

Authors: Xiang-Yu Wu, Han Gao, Charles Gale, Sangyong Jeon, Ralf Rapp

Event-by-event iEBE-MUSIC combines NLO QGP and in-medium hadronic emission with nonthermal rho sources for In+In and Ru+Ru. Low-mass and high-transverse-momentum dileptons may discriminate emission rates.

Assessment: A substantive multi-source calculation with comparisons to multiple observables. Low-mass spectra are broadly described, but intermediate-mass yields and some STAR flow lie below data; early-time radiation, viscous corrections and evolution uncertainties require further study. Rate sensitivity is a prediction, not a measurement of NLO effects.

Abstract summary

The authors calculate dilepton spectra and elliptic flow at SPS and RHIC energies, combining next-to-leading-order quark-gluon plasma radiation, in-medium hadronic radiation, and freeze-out and primordial rho decays, and compare with NA60 and preliminary STAR data.

Experiment | strong

Fluctuations and Correlations of Conserved Charges in Isobar Collisions at sqrt(sNN) = 200 GeV with the STAR Detector

Authors: Changfeng Li, Hanwen Feng (for the STAR Collaboration)

STAR reports second-order conserved-charge proxy correlations in Ru+Ru and Zr+Zr. The systems agree within uncertainties; a peripheral ordering matches lattice-motivated expectations but also appears in UrQMD without magnetic fields.

Assessment: The measurements use efficiency and centrality corrections and systematic variations, but finite acceptance and incomplete particle proxies limit direct thermodynamic comparison. Both lattice QCD and UrQMD are consistent with CBS despite differing predictions. The hierarchy alone is not evidence for magnetic fields.

Abstract summary

The authors report second-order cumulants and correlations of baryon number, electric charge and strangeness proxies in isobar collisions, compare baryon-strangeness correlation with lattice QCD and UrQMD, and study centrality dependence motivated by magnetic-field calculations.

AI/ML in physics | mixed

Improving Sensitivity to Rare Processes at the LHC with Geodesic-Aware Graph Neural Networks

Authors: Benjamin Fuks, Ahmed Hammad, Adil Jueid, Mihoko Nojiri

A geodesic-aware GNN maps radiation around a jet pair onto a sphere to identify colour flow; projected top-Higgs FCNC limits improve approximately tenfold relative to the authors' planar GNN. Possible heavy-ion transfer is jet radiation-pattern analysis, subject to large background and domain-shift tests.

Assessment: A physically motivated architecture with matched baseline and profile-likelihood nuisance parameters. The result is simulation-only, based on fast detector and leading-order background simulation; shower and hadronisation acceptance uncertainties, especially important for colour flow, are omitted. The large gain is not yet established in data or heavy-ion environments.

Abstract summary

The authors construct a spherical representation of colour-flow radiation and compare a geodesic-aware graph network to a planar baseline for simulated top-Higgs flavour-changing neutral-current searches, reporting expected limits under assumed luminosity and systematic uncertainties.
