Heavy-ion experiment: older context

Measurements of inclusive J/ψ production in Au+Au collisions at √sNN = 14.6, 17.3, 19.6, and 27 GeV with the STAR experiment

The STAR Collaboration (individual author count not verified). Submitted 19 November; listed 20 November.

Abstract summary: Inclusive midrapidity J/ψ nuclear modification is measured at four collision energies relative to interpolated pp references. Central RAA is approximately 0.5; energy dependence is not significant within uncertainties, while centrality and momentum dependences are suggestive.

Why it matters: Constraints on charmonium production across RHIC beam-energy-scan energies. Assessment: Strong measurement, but uncertainties, the interpolated reference, and hot and cold nuclear matter effects prevent a unique interpretation.

AI/ML in physics: older context

Who Moved My Z Boson? Revealing Resonance Features Inside a Neural Network

Kevin Alberto Urquía Calderón, Midori Kato, Inar Timiryasov, Oleg Ruchayskiy. Submitted 19 November; listed 20 November.

Abstract summary: Gradient-derived steering directions probe resonance features in a flow-matching collider event generator trained on ATLAS open data. Steering shifts the generated Z mass while much of the remainder of the dimuon spectrum changes little; ablation broadens the peak.

Use case and assessment: A substantive interpretability test with physical observables, potentially useful for auditing resonance modeling. This is a proof of concept: steering also broadens the peak and does not establish physically valid systematic variations or transfer to high-multiplicity heavy-ion events.
