Nuclear Modification of π⁰ Production in pO and OO Collisions with ALICE

Experiment · Nicolas Strangmann · arXiv:2611.34503

Summary: The OO-to-pO double ratio is suppressed by 4.9 standard deviations relative to an nNNPDF3.0 no-energy-loss baseline. The proceedings recaps an earlier ALICE measurement rather than presenting an independent new result.

Assessment: Strong experimental relevance; cold-matter cancellation depends on the baseline and different collision kinematics. The models shown do not resolve the energy-loss mechanism.

Abstract summary

Neutral-pion production in pO and OO collisions is measured with ALICE and compared with cold- and hot-matter calculations.

Accelerating ALICE background simulations: A WGAN Approach to slow-neutron induced TPC loopers

Heavy-ion AI/ML use · Marco Giacalone, Sandro Christian Wenzel, Mesut Arslandok, Andreas Morsch · arXiv:2611.34102

Summary: The model is integrated into ALICE simulation and the paper reports roughly tenfold acceleration of the relevant transport component, with comparisons to GEANT4.

Assessment: A practical, directly relevant application with production integration; the looper rate is separately normalized and momentum tails require attention. The timing gain is not an end-to-end speedup.

Abstract summary

A Wasserstein generative adversarial network generates electron and positron backgrounds from slow-neutron interactions in the ALICE TPC, replacing expensive transport.

Recent Developments in Colibri: A Neural-Network PDF Model and Improved Bayesian Sampling

Physics AI/ML · Ella Cole, Valentina Schütze Sánchez · arXiv:2611.34502

Summary: On 3,089 DIS pseudodata points, n3fit closure tests agree with NNPDF; the sampler comparison uses a 13-parameter polynomial model and yields consistent fit quality.

Assessment: A useful, quantitatively checked framework for controlled PDF methodology comparisons. The sampler benchmark is not an end-to-end Bayesian fit of the neural network, and direct nuclear-PDF application remains prospective.

Abstract summary

Colibri adds an open implementation of the n3fit neural-network PDF model and a BlackJAX nested sampler for comparing inference methods.
