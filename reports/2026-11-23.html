Soft–hard Bayesian inference in heavy-ion collisions

Theory · L. Du, S. A. Bass, M. Chartier, Y. Chen, Y.-C. Chen, et al. (51 authors; JETSCAPE)

In Pb–Pb collisions at 2.76 TeV, a three-parameter sequential inference combines a projected soft-sector posterior with high-pT hadron and inclusive-jet suppression. Medium geometry and jet–medium coupling compensate, shifting the preferred coupling relative to a fixed background.

Why it matters and assessment: Strong diagnostic of a consequential inference assumption, not a complete soft–hard calibration. The hard-only posterior reaches prior boundaries; only three parameters vary, the likelihood uses diagonal experimental variances and no model-discrepancy term, and other parameters are fixed.

Abstract summary

The authors combine JETSCAPE medium evolution and MATTER+LBT calculations with sequential Bayesian inference to examine medium–coupling correlations and the effect of propagating soft-sector information into high-pT suppression constraints.

Study of two-photon femtoscopic correlations in Ag+Ag collisions at √sNN = 2.55 GeV with HADES

Experiment · R. Abou Yassine, J. Adamczewski-Musch, C. Asal, et al. (121 authors; HADES Collaboration)

HADES measures one-dimensional two-photon correlations in Ag+Ag at 2.55 GeV and estimates upper limits on direct-photon correlation strength of order 10⁻⁴–10⁻³.

Why it matters and assessment: An experimentally valuable constraint on a difficult observable, with detector effects and systematic uncertainties addressed. It is an upper limit on correlation strength, not a direct-photon detection or a unique source-size measurement.

Abstract summary

Two-photon correlations complement charged-particle femtoscopy and can constrain low-pT direct photons. The study measures photon pairs with the HADES calorimeter, discusses observed correlation structures and estimates upper limits on the direct-photon correlation strength.
